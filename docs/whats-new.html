10 November 2026 ⋅ preprint
Composite local observables of sine-Gordon via stochastic analysis

With P. Paulovics (arXiv:2611.14296). A nonperturbative construction of local composite observables of the sine-Gordon model on the full plane up to \(\beta^2 < 6 \pi\), including vertex fields in the optimal Wick regime and derivatives of any order, combining renormalisation group ideas with stochastic analysis.

10 November 2026 ⋅ writing
Observables beyond the real line

The second post of the series on observables and self-adjoint operators: joint observables of commuting operators, normal operators and observables with values in manifolds, states as sources of probability laws, and what extra structure geometry and quantization require. A companion page gives the proofs and complements.

A constructive introduction to quantum field theory

What do we talk about when we talk about unbounded self-adjoint operators?

The first post of a series that uses the notion of an observable as an organising principle for the theory of unbounded self-adjoint operators: spectral measures, resolvents, unitary groups and strong resolvent convergence. A companion page gives the proofs and complements.
